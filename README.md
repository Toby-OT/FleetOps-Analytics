# FleetOps Analytics — Transportation & Logistics BI

An end-to-end Power BI business intelligence solution designed to provide centralized visibility into transportation and logistics operations, covering financial performance, delivery efficiency, driver safety, fleet maintenance, fuel consumption, routes, and customer profitability.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

FleetOps Analytics was developed to answer a core business question:

> **How can a logistics operation use its operational data to identify inefficiencies, manage risk, improve fleet performance, and protect profitability?**

The solution integrates multiple operational datasets into an interactive Power BI reporting environment and transforms raw operational data into actionable business intelligence.

### Key Business Areas

- Revenue and profitability
- Delivery performance
- Driver safety and operational risk
- Fuel consumption and efficiency
- Fleet maintenance
- Route performance
- Customer profitability
- Fleet utilization

---

## 🎯 Dashboard Modules

The report is structured into four analytical modules:

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Executive Overview | Tracks revenue, profitability, delivery performance, and operational KPIs |
| Driver & Safety Analytics | Monitors incidents, delivery efficiency, driver behaviour, and operational risk |
| Fleet & Maintenance Intelligence | Analyses fuel consumption, maintenance costs, and fleet utilization |
| Route & Customer Profitability | Evaluates profitable customers, freight categories, and strategic routes |

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Fuel is a major cost driver
Fuel costs exceeded **$95M**, making fuel optimization one of the most significant opportunities for reducing operating costs.

### 2. Extended Freight generated the highest revenue
Among the freight categories analysed, Extended Freight routes generated the highest revenue contribution.

### 3. Delivery performance requires attention
The overall on-time delivery rate remained below ideal operational benchmarks, indicating an opportunity to improve dispatching and route efficiency.

### 4. Safety risk was concentrated
Portland and Los Angeles recorded the highest incident volumes, highlighting locations that may require targeted safety interventions.

### 5. Maintenance costs were concentrated
A relatively small group of high-cost trucks accounted for a significant share of maintenance expenses, suggesting an opportunity for targeted maintenance planning.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project recommends:

- Implement predictive maintenance scheduling to reduce downtime and emergency repairs.
- Improve route optimization and dispatching to increase delivery efficiency.
- Introduce targeted safety programmes for high-risk drivers and routes.
- Reduce vehicle idle hours and prioritize fuel-efficient truck models.
- Continuously monitor customer and route profitability to improve operating margins.

## 📈 Business Value

The project demonstrates how operational data can move beyond descriptive reporting to support business decisions.

Instead of simply showing what happened, FleetOps Analytics connects operational metrics to questions around:

- Where are costs increasing?
- Which routes and customers are most valuable?
- Where are safety risks concentrated?
- Which fleet assets require attention?
- How can fuel consumption be reduced?
- Where can operational efficiency improve?
